```
Hueno is a camera colour identifier that tells you what colour something is, in words you already know. Point your phone at any object and read the colour name: "Dark blue", "Light green", "Reddish brown". The exact shade name and hex colour code sit right underneath.

Made for people who are colour blind, for anyone matching paint, fabric or makeup, and for artists working from real life.

WHAT COLOUR IS THIS?
• Live colour detector: point the camera and the colour name appears right away
• Simple colour names first (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, brown, grey, black, white), with light and dark shades
• A detailed colour name and hex code when you need the exact shade
• Tap to hear the colour name read aloud
• Steady readings: Hueno averages the area inside the ring, so the name does not flicker

KNOW WHEN TO TRUST THE READING
• One-tap white calibration: point at a sheet of white paper and tap. Hueno corrects for the light in your room
• Confidence indicator: Good, Fair or Poor, with a tip such as "Too dark, add light"
• Honest by design. A phone camera is not a lab instrument, and Hueno tells you when a reading is shaky

COMPARE TWO COLOURS
• Capture colour A, point at colour B, and Hueno says Same, Close or Different
• See how they differ: "B is a little lighter and more vivid"
• Handy for telling wires apart, checking status lights, pairing clothes, or holding a paint colour match against a wall

COLOUR PICKER FROM IMAGE
• Open any photo or screenshot and tap to pick a colour
• Zoom in and move the pointer to find the colour of a single pixel
• Copy the hex colour code with one tap

A COLOUR BLIND HELPER THAT SPEAKS PLAINLY
Many colour apps answer with names like "arsenic" or "camel". Hueno starts with the colour family, then the shade. If you are colour blind, red-green or any other type, you get an answer you can act on: which wire, which shirt, which line on the chart. Works with TalkBack and large text.

SAVE AND ORGANISE
• History of the colours you capture, each with its name and code
• Name your colours and group them into collections
• Export to CSV with Pro

RESPECTFUL ADS, FAIR UPGRADE

WHO USES HUENO
• Colour blind users who want a colour finder for everyday objects
• DIY and home projects: identify a paint colour before you shop
• Sewing, knitting and crafts: match thread, yarn and fabric
• Artists: find the true colour of shadows and skin tones in a reference
• Designers and developers: use colour recognition to grab a hex code from the real world

GOOD TO KNOW
Colour readings depend on your camera and lighting. For the most reliable result, use daylight or bright white light and calibrate first. Hueno is a reference tool. It is not a medical device and not a certified colour measurement instrument.
```
